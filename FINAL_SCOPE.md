# NeedFinder Global — Final scope

Business promise:
A customer anywhere can describe a legal product, service, property, vehicle, job, remote-work need, purchase or other requirement. NeedFinder organizes the request by country/city/category and connects the customer with suitable providers.

Core actors:
- Customer
- Provider/Seller
- Admin

Core marketplace:
- A–Z country directory
- Country + city
- Free-form need search
- Categories
- Provider listings
- Customer requirements
- Inquiries
- Reviews
- Verification/moderation foundation
- Commission-ready data model

Important:
The package is the complete source starter and deployment foundation. A real marketplace still needs the owner's external accounts (GitHub/Supabase/payment/notification providers) and their keys. Those cannot be pre-created inside a ZIP.
